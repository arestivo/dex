---
description: Content centred on the slide, for a single diagram, statement or quote.
---

## When to use

When a slide has one thing to show: a large diagram, a key statement, a single image. The heading, if any, is centred above it.

## Slots

- `title`: the slide's leading heading, centred.
- default: the centred content.

## Example

```markdown
::slide{#the-end layout=center}

# Questions?
```
